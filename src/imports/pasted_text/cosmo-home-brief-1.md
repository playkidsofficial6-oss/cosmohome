# COSMO HOME – Creative Direction & Homepage Experience Brief

## IMPORTANT

Do not create a clinic website.

Do not create a cosmetology website.

Do not create a medical website.

Do not create a premium template.

Do not create a luxury template.

COSMO HOME must feel like a unique luxury brand experience with its own personality, narrative, emotional appeal, and market positioning.

The homepage should tell a story.

Visitors should remember the feeling of the brand after leaving the website.

The goal is not to impress designers.

The goal is to make potential patients emotionally connect with COSMO HOME and feel that it is different from every other clinic in the market.

---

# BRAND POSITIONING

The biggest mistake aesthetic clinics make is selling treatments.

Everyone sells:

* Botox
* Fillers
* PRP
* Laser
* Facials

Nobody remembers that.

COSMO HOME should sell something deeper.

The brand should stand for:

Confidence.

Self-expression.

Self-care.

Natural beauty.

Personal transformation.

Expert-led aesthetic medicine.

The website should communicate:

"We do not change faces.

We help people become the best version of themselves."

---

# THE BIG IDEA

Create the entire homepage around one central concept:

## BEAUTY FEELS AT HOME

This should become the emotional foundation of the brand.

The word HOME in COSMO HOME is powerful.

Most clinics feel clinical.

Cold.

Transactional.

Medical.

COSMO HOME should feel:

Warm.

Personal.

Welcoming.

Exclusive.

Safe.

Luxurious.

Visitors should feel:

"This is where my beauty journey belongs."

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE

Primary:

Women aged 25–55.

Professionals.

Entrepreneurs.

Business owners.

Affluent clients.

Beauty-conscious individuals.

People who value quality over discounts.

People seeking trust rather than the cheapest treatment.

---

Curiosity

↓

↓

Aspiration

↓

Consultation Booking

Every section must contribute to this journey.

# HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

## CHAPTER 1

THE FIRST IMPRESSION

Do not create a traditional hero section.

Create a cinematic opening scene.

Large editorial typography.

Full-screen luxury composition.

Headline:

Beauty Feels At Home.

Subheadline:

Expert-led aesthetic medicine designed around confidence, care, and natural beauty.

Dr. Ruxana should appear as a central figure within the visual composition.

Not just a photograph.

She should feel like the face of the brand.

Create emotional impact before selling anything.

---

## CHAPTER 2

THE PHILOSOPHY

Instead of an About section.

Tell the philosophy behind COSMO HOME.

Explain:

Why it exists.

What it believes.

Why beauty should feel personal.

Why expertise matters.

Create an emotional connection.

---

## CHAPTER 3

MEET DR. RUXANA

Not a biography.

Not a resume.

Tell her story.

Show:

Vision.

Passion.

Expertise.

Achievements.

Approach.

Create authority through storytelling.

Visitors should feel:

"I trust this doctor."

---

## CHAPTER 4

THE ART OF TRANSFORMATION

Do not immediately show treatments.

Show outcomes.

Show confidence.

Show transformation stories.

Focus on people.

Not procedures.

Not technology.

Not machines.

People.

---

## CHAPTER 5

SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES

Do not call them treatments.

Call them experiences.

Examples:

Skin Renewal Experience

Age Gracefully Experience

Confidence Restoration Experience

Hair Revival Experience

Personalized Aesthetic Journey

Position them as premium experiences.

Not medical procedures.

---

## CHAPTER 6

THE COSMO HOME JOURNEY

Show the complete experience.

Arrival.

Consultation.

Planning.

Treatment.

Aftercare.

Results.

Make visitors imagine themselves there.

---

## CHAPTER 7

REAL STORIES

Not testimonials.

Stories.

People remember stories.

Show emotional journeys.

Before.

During.

After.

Confidence gained.

Problems solved.

Life improved.

---

## CHAPTER 8

INVITATION

Do not create a sales section.

Create an invitation.

Headline:

Your Journey Begins Here.

Subheadline:

Book a private consultation with Dr. Ruxana and discover what personalized aesthetic care feels like.

Make the visitor feel welcomed.

Not sold to.

---

# MOBILE EXPERIENCE

This website will primarily be viewed on mobile.

Design mobile first.

The mobile version should feel like a premium beauty application.

Not a compressed desktop website.

Every interaction should feel effortless and luxurious.

---

# FINAL GOAL

When visitors leave the homepage, they should remember one thing:

COSMO HOME is not another clinic.

It is a luxury destination where beauty, confidence, expertise, and care come together.

The website should feel like a brand people want to belong to, not simply a place where they book a treatment.
